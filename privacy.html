Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Letterfly processes, why we process it, and how users can exercise their privacy rights.

Effective August 19, 2026

1. Information We Process

  • Authentication information, including Google account identifiers, email addresses, and Firebase UIDs
  • Profile information, including nickname, date of birth, calculated age and age group, gender, and country or region
  • Firefly letters, conversation messages, read status, and conversation status
  • Reports, blocks, enforcement actions, appeals, and related review records
  • Purchase and receipt verification information, paid entitlement balances, and subscription status
  • Push notification tokens, last activity time, app version information, error logs, and security logs

2. How We Use Information

We process information to authenticate users, manage accounts, separate age groups, provide matching and messaging, deliver push notifications, enforce sending limits, manage paid features, verify purchases, process reports and blocks, apply safety restrictions, prevent abuse and fraud, provide customer support, maintain service reliability, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Messages and Report Evidence

Messages may be stored as necessary to provide conversations through Letterfly. When a report is submitted, relevant conversation content may be preserved separately as an evidence snapshot for safety reviews, appeals, dispute resolution, or other legitimate enforcement purposes.

4. Automated Systems and AI Analysis

Letterfly may use automated systems or artificial intelligence to assist with safety and moderation reviews. When an automated decision materially affects a user's access to the service, Letterfly may provide information about the decision, relevant evidence, an appeal process, and human review where appropriate.

5. Service Providers and International Processing

Letterfly relies on third-party service providers to operate certain features. These may include Google Cloud and Firebase for infrastructure and application services, Vertex AI (Gemini) for certain automated safety functions, Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution and payment-related services, and other analytics or service providers where applicable.

Depending on the service provider and the user's location, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country in which the user resides. We take reasonable measures to use service providers and safeguards appropriate to the information being processed and applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account and profile information is generally retained while an account remains active. Ordinary conversation data may be retained as necessary to provide the service or until it is deleted in accordance with Letterfly's functionality and policies.

Certain records, including safety reports, enforcement records, appeals, payment or refund records, and information related to disputes, may be retained separately for the period reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

When information is no longer required for the purposes for which it was retained, it will be deleted or otherwise disposed of using reasonable measures designed to prevent unauthorized recovery.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain processing.

You may manage or delete your account through the Letterfly app or follow the instructions on our Account Deletion page.

8. Protection of Minors

Letterfly does not permit users under the age of 14 to create an account. New connections between users in the minor age group and users in the adult age group are separated by the service.

For more information, please review our Child Safety Policy.
