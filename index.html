How it works

The letter comes first.

Letterfly is designed around one-to-one text conversations rather than public feeds, follower counts, or profile-photo-based discovery.

1

Write a letter

Write an opening message of at least 30 characters and choose the available recipient preferences. Letterfly applies safety and usage rules to new messages before delivery.

2

Send a firefly

Your firefly carries the letter to one eligible adult recipient according to your selected conditions and Letterfly's service rules. Delivery or a reply is not guaranteed.

3

Take turns replying

When a conversation begins, messaging is designed around one reply at a time. Waiting for the other person's words is part of the Letterfly experience.

Safety and enforcement

Keeping the forest safer.

Letterfly provides in-app reporting and blocking tools for user-generated conversations. Users can report another user or relevant conversation content, and can block another user to restrict further interaction.

Letterfly also uses moderation systems intended to identify abusive, harmful, or unsafe content. Certain new first-contact messages and content associated with reports may be analyzed as part of the safety review process.

When a violation is confirmed, Letterfly may apply restrictions, suspend access, terminate an account, or take other proportionate safety measures based on the nature and history of the violation.

Where an enforcement decision is eligible for appeal, Letterfly may provide an appeal process and human review.

Adults only

Users under 18 are not permitted to create an account, access, or use Letterfly. Date-of-birth and age-eligibility information are used to help enforce this restriction.
