How it works

The letter comes first.

Instead of a feed made to be seen, Letterfly creates a simple space where you can focus on one person at a time.

1

Write a letter

Write an opening message of at least 30 characters and choose your preferred recipient conditions. Contact information, external links, and account IDs cannot be included.

2

Send a firefly

Your letter is carried to one person who matches your selected conditions. If an unopened letter remains unread for a certain period of time, it may be sent again under the same conditions.

3

Take turns replying

Conversations move one reply at a time. Waiting for the other person's words is part of the Letterfly experience.

Built for focus

Only what matters.

One-to-one text

No public posts or follower counts. Letterfly is designed around words, replies, and one conversation at a time.

Daily sending limits

Random delivery and selected-condition delivery are managed separately, while excessive mass sending is limited.

Unread letters first

The main forest highlights fireflies you have not yet opened, keeping your experience simple and focused.

Safety and enforcement

Keeping the forest safe.

Reported conversations may be reviewed together with relevant evidence. When a violation is confirmed, restrictions may be applied based on the nature and history of the violation. Users placed under a shadow restriction are shown a dedicated experience and may only form new connections with other users under the same status.

When an automated decision affects access to the service, Letterfly provides an appeal process and the possibility of human review.
