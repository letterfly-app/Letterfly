Child Safety

Child Safety Policy

Letterfly does not tolerate the sexual exploitation or abuse of children or minors in any form.

Effective August 19, 2026
If someone is in immediate danger
Do not wait for an in-app report to be reviewed. Contact local law enforcement, emergency services, or the appropriate child protection authority immediately.

1. Age Restrictions and Separation

  • Users under the age of 14 are not permitted to create an account or use Letterfly.
  • Users aged 14–17 may only be newly connected with other users within the minor age group.
  • Users aged 18 or older may only be newly connected with users within the adult age group.
  • We may restrict access when we suspect that a user has provided a false date of birth or attempted to bypass age-group restrictions.

2. Strictly Prohibited Conduct

Letterfly strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, requests for sexual conversations, images, videos, or meetings involving minors, and any attempt to solicit, threaten, trade, possess, distribute, or facilitate content or activity for the purpose of sexual exploitation. Content that depicts, promotes, encourages, or facilitates the sexual exploitation of children is prohibited even when it does not involve real imagery.

3. Reporting and Safety Measures

When a relevant report is received, Letterfly may preserve necessary evidence and take immediate safety measures, including restricting accounts or access to the service. If we become aware of illegal child sexual abuse material or a credible risk of harm to a child, we will follow applicable legal reporting requirements and cooperate with relevant authorities as required by law.

4. Protection of Potential Victims

We do not place responsibility on users who may have experienced abuse or exploitation, and we do not require them to repeatedly submit information that is not necessary to review the incident. Reports and related evidence are handled with restricted access and only by those who require access for safety, investigation, or legal purposes.
