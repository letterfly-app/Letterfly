Child Safety

Child Safety Policy

Letterfly is an adults-only service and does not tolerate the sexual exploitation or abuse of children or minors in any form.

Effective August 23, 2026
If someone is in immediate danger
Do not wait for an in-app report to be reviewed. Contact local law enforcement, emergency services, or the appropriate child protection authority immediately.

1. Adults-Only Service

  • Letterfly is intended only for adults aged 18 or older.
  • Users under the age of 18 are not permitted to create an account, access, or use Letterfly.
  • Users must provide accurate age and date-of-birth information when requested by the service.
  • Users may not misrepresent their age, provide a false date of birth, or attempt to bypass any age-related restriction or age-assurance measure.
  • Letterfly may restrict, suspend, or terminate access if we reasonably believe that an account is being used by a person under the age of 18 or that an age restriction has been circumvented.

2. Strictly Prohibited Conduct

Letterfly strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation involving minors, requests for sexual conversations, images, videos, or meetings involving minors, and any attempt to solicit, threaten, trade, possess, distribute, create, or facilitate content or activity for the purpose of sexual exploitation of a child.

Content that depicts, promotes, encourages, normalizes, or facilitates the sexual exploitation of children is prohibited even when it does not involve real imagery.

3. Reports and Immediate Safety Measures

When a relevant report or safety signal is received, Letterfly may preserve necessary evidence and take immediate safety measures, including restricting, suspending, or terminating accounts or access to the service.

If we become aware that an account may be operated by a minor, or that a user may be attempting to contact, exploit, groom, or otherwise harm a minor, we may take action without waiting for the completion of an ordinary enforcement process where necessary for safety.

If we become aware of illegal child sexual abuse material or a credible risk of harm to a child, we will follow applicable legal reporting requirements and cooperate with relevant authorities as required by law.

4. Handling of Child Safety Evidence

Reports and related evidence concerning child safety are handled with restricted access and only by persons or service providers who require access for safety, investigation, enforcement, or legal purposes.

We may retain relevant records for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate potential abuse, prevent repeat violations, comply with legal obligations, cooperate with authorities, or protect users and others.

5. If a Minor Accesses Letterfly

If we become aware that a person under the age of 18 has created or used a Letterfly account in violation of our eligibility requirements, we may restrict or terminate that account and take appropriate steps regarding the associated personal information in accordance with applicable law, our Privacy Policy, and our safety obligations.

A parent, guardian, or other person who believes that a minor has accessed Letterfly may contact us using the child safety contact information below.
