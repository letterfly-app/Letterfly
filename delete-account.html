Account Deletion

Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the Letterfly app. If you are unable to access the app, you may request account deletion through our support team.

Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Sign in to your Letterfly account.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Usage → Delete Account & Data.
  3. Complete the confirmation process and approve the final deletion.

Simply uninstalling Letterfly from your device does not delete your account or data stored on our servers.

If You Cannot Access the App

Contact us at the email address below with the subject “Letterfly Account Deletion Request.” To help us verify account ownership, we recommend contacting us from the email address associated with your Letterfly account whenever possible.

Request Deletion by Email

Information That Will Be Deleted

  • Account identifiers and profile information
  • Regular conversations and messages created by the user
  • Messaging limits and device or push notification connection information
  • Unused in-app entitlement or credit balances associated with the account

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information, including report evidence, enforcement and appeal records, payment or refund records, and information related to legal disputes, may be retained separately for the minimum period necessary for safety, legal obligations, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Such information will be deleted when the applicable retention purpose no longer exists.

After Your Request Is Processed

Once an in-app deletion request is successfully completed, access to the account will end. For deletion requests submitted by email, we may verify account ownership before confirming the result of the request. App Store or Google Play subscriptions are managed separately from your Letterfly account and must be canceled through the respective app store.
