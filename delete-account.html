Account Deletion

Account and Data Deletion

Letterfly allows users to permanently delete their account directly from the app. Account deletion removes the Letterfly account and associated personal data that Letterfly is not legally required to retain.

Effective August 25, 2026

1. Delete Your Account in the Letterfly App

You can initiate permanent account deletion directly within Letterfly.

  1. Sign in to the Letterfly account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Account & Usage → Delete Account & Data .
  3. Review the deletion information shown in the app.
  4. Complete the confirmation process and approve the final deletion.

Account deletion is permanent. Deleting your account is different from logging out, uninstalling the app, or temporarily stopping use of Letterfly.

Letterfly does not require users to contact customer support in order to delete an account when they can access the in-app deletion feature.

2. Uninstalling the App Does Not Delete Your Account

Removing Letterfly from your device does not delete your Letterfly account or personal information stored on Letterfly's systems.

To permanently delete your account, use the Delete Account & Data function inside the Letterfly app.

3. If You Cannot Access the App

If you are unable to access Letterfly because of a technical or account-access problem, you may contact Letterfly support for assistance with the deletion process.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you are the owner of the account before acting on a support request.

When possible, contact us from the email address associated with your Letterfly account.

Contact Account Support

Support contact is provided as an alternative for users who cannot access the in-app deletion function. It is not required for users who can delete their account directly in the app.

4. Information Deleted With Your Account

When account deletion is completed, Letterfly deletes personal information associated with the account that is no longer required for a legitimate or legally required retention purpose.

This generally includes:

  • Letterfly account identifiers and active profile information;
  • Nickname and profile preferences;
  • Date-of-birth and ordinary age-eligibility information associated with the active account;
  • Ordinary conversation and message data associated with the account, except information that must be retained for a permitted legal, safety, security, or dispute-resolution purpose;
  • Push notification tokens and ordinary device connection information associated with the account;
  • Account-linked usage information that is no longer required for security or legal purposes;
  • Letterfly-managed balances or entitlements associated with the deleted account, subject to applicable storefront records and legal requirements.

5. Information That May Be Retained

Letterfly may retain limited information after account deletion only where reasonably necessary for a legitimate retention purpose or where required by applicable law.

This may include:

  • Safety reports and relevant evidence needed to investigate serious abuse or protect users;
  • Enforcement and appeal records;
  • Security, fraud-prevention, and abuse-prevention records;
  • Records necessary to prevent circumvention of account restrictions or age restrictions;
  • Transaction, refund, chargeback, or accounting records that must be retained for legal or financial compliance;
  • Information subject to a valid legal preservation obligation, dispute, investigation, or lawful government request.

Retained information is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and is deleted or otherwise disposed of when that retention purpose ends, subject to applicable law.

6. What Happens After Deletion

When an in-app account deletion is successfully completed, access to the deleted Letterfly account ends.

Some backend deletion or cleanup operations may require additional processing time. If additional action from you is required, Letterfly will provide appropriate information.

Deleted accounts cannot be restored through the normal Letterfly account-recovery process. If you later return to Letterfly, you may need to create a new account.

7. App Store and Google Play Purchases

Deleting your Letterfly account does not erase transaction records maintained independently by Apple or Google.

Purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play remain subject to the applicable storefront's payment, transaction, refund, and record-retention policies.

For additional information about purchases, see our Terms of Service and Refund Policy.

8. Account Deletion Does Not Cancel a Subscription

Deleting your Letterfly account does not automatically cancel an active Ad-Free Pass subscription.

The Ad-Free Pass subscription is managed by the storefront through which it was purchased.

Apple App Store

If you subscribed on iPhone or iPad, manage or cancel your subscription using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Subscriptions can generally be managed through:

Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Letterfly

Google Play

If you subscribed through Google Play, manage or cancel the subscription through Google Play using the Google account that made the purchase.

Cancel the subscription separately if you do not want future renewal charges.

9. Sign in With Apple or Google

Deleting a Letterfly account removes the Letterfly account associated with the applicable authentication identity.

Authentication providers such as Apple or Google may independently retain information associated with your Apple Account or Google Account according to their own policies.

Deleting a Letterfly account does not delete your Apple Account, Google Account, or other account maintained by an external authentication provider.

10. Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have additional rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other processing of your personal information.

For more information about how Letterfly handles personal data, please review our Privacy Policy.
