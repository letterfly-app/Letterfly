Refund Policy

This policy explains cancellation, refund, and entitlement rules for Letterfly's digital purchases and auto-renewable subscription.

Effective August 25, 2026

1. Purchases Through the App Store or Google Play

Digital purchases made in Letterfly are processed through the storefront used to make the purchase, such as the Apple App Store or Google Play.

The applicable storefront processes the payment and generally determines refund eligibility according to its policies and applicable law. Letterfly does not independently process your App Store or Google Play payment-card information.

A purchase being eligible for a refund is not guaranteed. Eligibility may depend on the transaction, circumstances of the request, storefront policy, and applicable consumer law.

2. Apple App Store Refund Requests

If you purchased a Letterfly product through the Apple App Store, refund requests should be submitted to Apple.

Apple provides its refund-request service at: reportaproblem.apple.com .

Apple determines whether an App Store transaction qualifies for a refund under Apple's policies and applicable law.

Letterfly support may assist with identifying a transaction or investigating a technical problem, but Letterfly cannot guarantee or independently approve a refund for a transaction processed by Apple.

3. Google Play Refund Requests

If you purchased a Letterfly product through Google Play, refund requests are generally handled through Google Play according to Google's policies and applicable law.

Letterfly support may assist with technical or entitlement problems, but refund eligibility for transactions handled by Google Play is subject to the applicable Google Play rules and payment process.

4. Consumable Digital Credits

Letterfly may offer consumable digital credits, including credits for additional new firefly sends or an additional message in an existing conversation.

Consumable credits are deducted when successfully used. Used credits cannot normally be restored for reuse.

Purchased credits are separate from Letterfly's periodically refreshed free allowances. Purchased credits do not expire merely because a daily free allowance resets.

If a purchase is refunded, reversed, or otherwise invalidated by the applicable storefront, Letterfly may remove unused credits or other entitlements that were granted by that transaction, where permitted by applicable law and storefront rules.

5. Undelivered or Incorrect Purchases

If payment was successfully completed but a purchased product or entitlement does not appear in Letterfly, first use any available Restore Purchases function where applicable.

If the problem continues, contact Letterfly support and provide enough transaction information for us to investigate.

Depending on the circumstances, Letterfly may attempt to restore the correct entitlement or direct you to the applicable storefront's refund or payment-support process.

6. Ad-Free Pass Subscription

Ad-Free Pass is Letterfly's optional auto-renewable monthly subscription.

  • Subscription title: Ad-Free Pass
  • Subscription period: One month
  • Benefit: Standard Letterfly advertisements are removed while the subscription is active.
  • Price: The current localized price is displayed in Letterfly and by the applicable storefront before purchase confirmation.

The Ad-Free Pass automatically renews for additional one-month periods unless it is cancelled through the applicable storefront.

Renewal pricing and billing are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play according to the storefront through which you subscribed.

7. Cancelling Ad-Free Pass

Cancelling the Ad-Free Pass prevents future automatic renewals. Cancellation does not normally end the already-paid subscription period immediately.

After cancellation, Ad-Free Pass benefits generally remain available until the end of the current paid billing period.

On iPhone or iPad

An App Store subscription can generally be managed through:

Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Letterfly

You may also manage subscriptions using Apple's subscription-management options.

On Android

A Google Play subscription can generally be managed through the subscriptions section of Google Play using the Google account that made the purchase.

8. Cancellation Is Different From a Refund

Cancelling an auto-renewable subscription prevents future renewal charges. It does not by itself create a refund for a previously completed payment.

If you want to request a refund for a completed transaction, you must use the refund process provided by the storefront through which the purchase was made.

9. Deleting the App or Your Letterfly Account

Uninstalling Letterfly does not cancel an active subscription.

Deleting your Letterfly account also does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.

If you no longer want an Ad-Free Pass subscription to renew, cancel it separately through the Apple App Store or Google Play using the account that made the purchase.

For information about deleting your Letterfly account, see our Account Deletion page.

10. Effect of a Refund or Charge Reversal

If a purchase is refunded, reversed, cancelled by the payment provider, or otherwise invalidated, Letterfly may remove the digital benefit associated with that transaction.

This may include:

  • Unused purchased credits;
  • An active subscription entitlement;
  • Other digital benefits granted specifically by the refunded transaction.

Letterfly will not remove an entitlement in a manner prohibited by applicable law or storefront rules.

11. Information Needed for Payment Support

If you contact Letterfly about a payment or entitlement problem, providing the following information may help us investigate:

  • Order or transaction number;
  • Approximate purchase date and time;
  • Name of the purchased product;
  • Email address associated with your Letterfly account, where applicable;
  • Screenshots showing the purchase or technical error, where useful.

Do not send your password, Apple Account password, Google Account password, full payment-card number, card security code, banking password, or other sensitive payment credentials.

12. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to limit any refund, cancellation, statutory warranty, or other consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

Where applicable law provides greater rights than this policy, those legal rights apply.
