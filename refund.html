Refund Policy

This policy explains the cancellation and refund rules for Letterfly's consumable digital items and monthly subscription products.

Effective August 23, 2026

1. General Policy

Purchases, cancellations, and refunds are primarily governed by the policies of the app store through which the purchase was made, including the Apple App Store and Google Play, as well as applicable law.

Refund requests should generally be submitted through the purchase history, subscription management, or customer support process provided by the applicable app store. Approval of a refund may be determined by the applicable app store in accordance with its policies.

2. Consumable Digital Items

Additional Send Passes and Send Again Passes are consumable digital items that are deducted when used. Items that have already been successfully used are generally not eligible for a refund, except where otherwise required by applicable law or the policies of the applicable app store.

3. Reasons a Refund May Be Considered

  • A duplicate charge was made for the same transaction
  • A purchased item was not delivered after payment was completed
  • A technical error prevented the purchased item or service from being used normally
  • The purchase was unauthorized or another refund right applies under applicable law or store policy

4. Ad-Free Subscription

The Letterfly Ad-Free subscription is an automatically renewing monthly subscription priced at US$3.99. The final price displayed to users may vary by country, currency, taxes, and the pricing applied by the relevant app store.

You may cancel the subscription at any time through the Apple App Store or Google Play account through which it was purchased. After cancellation, subscription benefits generally remain available until the end of the already-paid billing period.

Unless cancelled before the next renewal date, the subscription may automatically renew in accordance with the terms shown by the applicable app store. Uninstalling or deleting the Letterfly app does not automatically cancel a subscription.

5. Entitlements After a Refund

If a purchase is refunded, any unused entitlement, digital item balance, or subscription benefit granted through that purchase may be revoked or removed from the account.

6. Information for Payment Support

To help us review a payment issue, please provide the order or transaction number, purchase date and time, purchased product, the email address associated with your Letterfly account where applicable, and any relevant screenshots showing the error.

Do not send your password, full payment card number, security code, bank account password, or other sensitive payment credentials.
